Do’s (Responsible Practices)
Think before posting – Ensure your content is appropriate and respectful.
Maintain privacy settings – Keep your personal information secure.
Be respectful and positive – Engage in healthy and constructive communication.
Verify information before sharing – Avoid spreading fake news or misinformation.
Build a professional image – Share achievements, skills, and meaningful content.

Don’ts (Things to Avoid)
Don’t share sensitive information – Avoid posting personal details like address, phone number, or passwords.
Don’t engage in cyberbullying – Avoid offensive, harmful, or abusive comments.
Don’t overshare personal life – Maintain a balance between online and offline life.
Don’t post inappropriate content – Avoid content that can harm your reputation.
Don’t trust unknown links or profiles – Be cautious of scams and phishing attempts.
