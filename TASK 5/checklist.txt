Stay Safe Online – Checklist
 Prevention Tips
Use strong and unique passwords – Combine letters, numbers, and symbols; avoid using the same password for multiple accounts.
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) – Add an extra layer of security to email, social media, and banking apps.
Do not share OTPs or passwords with anyone – Banks or officials never ask for these details. (UPI/Financial Safety)
Verify UPI payment requests carefully – Always check the sender’s name before approving any payment request. (UPI/Financial Safety)
Avoid clicking on suspicious links or emails – Double-check URLs before entering login details.
Use secure Wi-Fi networks only – Avoid logging into important accounts on public Wi-Fi.
Keep your devices updated – Install security updates and use antivirus software.
Limit personal information on social media – Avoid sharing location, phone numbers, or sensitive details publicly.
Log out from shared devices – Especially in cyber cafes or college labs.
Download apps only from trusted sources – Use official app stores like Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
